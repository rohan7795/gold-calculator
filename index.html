Useful formula

Net gold weight = gross weight − stone/non-gold weight.
Total weight = gross weight of the jewellery.
Gold value = net gold weight × gold rate.
Making charges are calculated on total weight value (or total weight when using ₹/g), not just the net gold weight.
GST = GST% × (gold value + making charges).
Extra to pay = making charges + GST; total amount you pay = gold value + making charges + GST.
Old gold sell value = old gold weight × old gold purity × old gold rate.
Extra amount to pay out of pocket = total amount you pay (new jewellery) − old gold sell value.
